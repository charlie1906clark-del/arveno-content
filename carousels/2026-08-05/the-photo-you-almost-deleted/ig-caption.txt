progress photos you hate taking

You take it on a bad morning, in bad light, and your thumb goes straight to the bin.

Take it anyway. It is not for today.

Here is the thing about mirrors: you see yourself in one every single day, so the version in your head updates every single day too. Change that happens slowly is invisible to a memory that keeps being overwritten. That is not a mindset problem, it is just how looking at yourself works.

A photo does not update. It sits in the camera roll being honest about a specific Tuesday, and in eight weeks it is the only record you have of where you started.

The one you nearly deleted is usually the one that ends up mattering.

When did you last take one?
