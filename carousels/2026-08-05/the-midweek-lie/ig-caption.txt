why we always restart on a monday

"I'll start again Monday."

Said on a Wednesday, that sentence costs five days — and worse, you spend those five days feeling bad about a week you have already decided to write off. The guilt does not buy you anything. It just makes the restart heavier.

Monday is not a fresh start. It is a date on a calendar. Your body has no idea what day it is; it responds to what you actually did, and it counts a Wednesday exactly the same as a Monday.

You do not need a clean slate. You need the next hour.

What are you doing with today?
