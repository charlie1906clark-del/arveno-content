how to actually check in on your body

Most weekly check-ins are a guess. You look in the mirror, try to remember last Wednesday, and grade the week on how you happen to feel this morning.

Here is what Arveno does instead. One photo, taken in your own room, under 1 minute. You get a directional read on your body composition that you can track as a trend rather than a single verdict.

And here is the part most apps leave out: it is an estimate. Photo-based body composition has decent accuracy across a group and real limits on any one person, so we present it as a trend to follow, not a clinical fact. It is not a DEXA scan and it is not a medical measurement.

We are in open beta, built by one founder, and things will change under you. If that sounds like the right trade, the first scan is free.
