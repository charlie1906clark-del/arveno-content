why monday is the worst day to start

Nobody actually starts on a Monday. Monday is when we announce it. People start on the day it finally annoys them enough — the jeans, the stairs, the photo, the tired that will not shift.

And that day is almost never convenient. It is raining, it is late, you are already knackered. That is what starting actually looks like: unglamorous, badly timed, and real.

Waiting for a clean slate is just waiting with extra steps. The day you begin is whichever one you decide counts.
